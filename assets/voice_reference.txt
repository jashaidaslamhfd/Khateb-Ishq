kaha tha na. .. !

mujhe tum is tarhan sotay hue mat chor kar jana. .

mujhe be shak jaga dena ,

bta dena. .

mohabbat ke safar mein

sath mere chal nahi sakteen ,

judai ke, behar mein tum

mere sang jal nahi sakteen. .

tumhe rasta badalna hai ,

meri had se niklana hai. .

tumhe kis baat ka dar tha ?

tumhe jaanay nahi deta ?

kahin par qaid kar laita ?

arrey pagal !

mohabbat ki tabiyat mein zabardasti nahi hoti. .

jisay rasta badalna ho ,

usay rasta badlne se. .

jisay had se niklana ho ,

usay had se niklny se. .

nah koi rokkk paaya hai ,

nah koi rokkk paye ga. .

tumhe kis baat ka dar tha ?

mujhe be shak jaga detin ,

mein tum ko dekh hi laita !

tumhe koi dua deta !

kam az kam yun to nah hota. .

mere saathi! haqeeqat hai. .

tumahray baad khonay ke liye kuch bhi nahi baqi ,

magar. .. .

khonay se darta hon. .

mein ab sonay se darta hon. .. .! !
